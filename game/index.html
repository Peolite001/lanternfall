The rules of the pier

  1. Flame is life. Both keepers begin at 30. Take the other to 0.
  2. Oil pays for cards. You gain 1 each turn, up to 10, and it refills each turn.
  3. Keepers sit in one of three beacons. At the end of your turn each strikes the Keeper facing it, or the enemy Flame if that beacon is open.
  4. Beacon tides. A Keeper whose tide matches its beacon's gains +1 Power.
  5. Spring Tide every third round: every Keeper strikes twice. Save your swings for it.
  6. Charms burn, lift, or thicken a Keeper. An empty deck costs 3 Flame a turn.
